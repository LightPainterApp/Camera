Light Painting Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 17, 2026

Policy Details

  1. Camera Access: The app needs access to the camera to fulfill its main purpose of taking photos.
  2. Storage Access: The app needs access to the device storage in order to save the photos you take.
  3. Directory Permissions: The app only accesses (reads and writes) files in the directory you designate (e.g., /DCIM, /DCIM/100_CFV5, or a custom location).
  4. Internal Configurations: Settings and internal configurations are saved to the private portion of the internal storage dedicated to the app[cite: 2].
  5. Geolocation: You may optionally add geolocation information to your photos’ metadata using GPS and cellular location. This is disabled by default[cite: 2].
  6. Fused Location Provider: This feature uses Google Mobile Services and is subject to their privacy policy. Your location is not shared unless you actively share photos containing that metadata[cite: 2].
  7. Pricing: The app is offered for a low price on the Google Play Store[cite: 2].
  8. Usage Statistics: The app may collect anonymous error reports and Log Data (device name, OS version, etc.) to help fix issues. Users in the EEA must provide explicit consent (opt-in), while others can opt-out in Settings[cite: 2].
  9. Cookies: While the app does not use cookies explicitly, third-party libraries may. Opting out of statistics and error reports will disable these[cite: 2].
